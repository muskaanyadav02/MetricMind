# Metric Dictionary

This document defines the official business metrics used by MetricMind.

## Revenue

Net sales after applicable discounts and refunds.

## Gross Sales

Quantity × Unit Price

## Total Cost

Material Cost + Shipping Cost + Labor Cost + Other Cost

## Gross Profit

Revenue - Total Cost

## Gross Margin

Gross Profit / Revenue × 100

## Orders

Count of distinct completed orders.

## Average Order Value

Revenue / Orders

## Return Rate

Returned Orders / Completed Orders × 100
